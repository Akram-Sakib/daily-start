# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project about a morning checklist — there
is nothing here worth being unpleasant over.

## What that means in practice

Expected:

- Assume the other person is trying to help, including when they are wrong.
- Keep criticism on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that "no thanks, out of scope" is a legitimate answer, and that
  maintainers may be slow. This is spare-time work.
- Remember that most people here are not writing in their first language.
  Read past awkward phrasing rather than scoring points off it.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Comments demeaning people over gender, gender identity, sexual orientation,
  disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or
  nationality.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled arguments, or pressuring people for
  free work.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, commit messages,
and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Email <sayedakramsakib@gmail.com> with `daily-start conduct` in the subject.
Reports go only to me. I will keep the reporter's identity private.

If the report concerns my own behaviour, you are welcome to say so publicly
in an issue — I would rather be told than not.

## Enforcement

I am the only maintainer, so this is not a committee. Depending on what
happened I may: let it go with a word, edit or delete a comment, ask someone
to step back from a thread, or block them from the repository. Serious or
repeated behaviour goes straight to a block.

I will explain the reason for any action taken, unless doing so would expose
the person who reported it.
