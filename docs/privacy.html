Privacy Policy — Daily Start

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Daily Start does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It makes no network requests of any kind.

What the app stores

Everything you type into Daily Start — your name, your routine list, your daily checklist items and which ones you ticked off — is written to a single file on your own computer:

%APPDATA%\Daily Start\daily-start.json

You can open, read, edit, back up or delete that file at any time. Uninstalling the app leaves the file in place; delete that folder to remove your data completely.

What the app sends

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The app works with no internet connection at all. Even the typefaces it uses are bundled inside the app rather than fetched from a font service.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The developer has no access to your data, because your data never leaves your device. There is no server to send it to.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever adds a feature that sends data somewhere — for example optional sync between your own computers — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and such a feature will be off until you switch it on yourself.
